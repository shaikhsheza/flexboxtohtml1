nepal

nepal is a landlocked country in south asia, located between india and tibet. it's largely mountainous and famous for its himalayas. but it has a very diverse landscape and differences through season and altitude. the southern tropical plains are humid, while there is a cold arctic region in the north.

this country ranked 4th globally in vulnerability to climate change. natural hazards and disasters nepal already faces are getting intensified by global warming, there is rising temperatures, species shifting, glacier retreat, and more frequent extreme precipitation.

last month, near the border of china, a glacial collaps and avalance caused seismic waves and a massive flash flood, devastating this portion of nepal. scientists observed exceptional heat, then destabilized rock from thawing permafrost, in the days leading up to the event.

ways to minimize the impact of climate change in this area

  • conserving biodiversity
  • helping people and ecosystems build resilience and adapt to climate change
  • mitigating greenhouse gas emissions

what are local people doing?

currently, the country is trying to restore itself after the disaster last month. responses to climate change locally include: improving irrigation, conserving water sources, planting trees, introducing improved seeds and crops, protecting drinking-water systems and changing livestock practices.
